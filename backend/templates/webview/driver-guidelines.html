Vehicle

Keep the car ready

Your vehicle should look clean, feel safe, and match your approved profile.

  • ✓Clean seats, floor, boot, and exterior before going online.
  • ✓Keep registration, insurance, and plate details current.
  • ✓Do not accept rides with warning lights or unsafe tyres.
Passenger care

Confirm every trip

Start each ride with a quick check so the rider feels safe and the route is correct.

  • ✓Confirm the rider name and destination before moving.
  • ✓Use the in-app route unless campus safety redirects you.
  • ✓Keep calls, music, and conversations professional.
Garage rides

Boarding discipline

Garage ride QR boarding must stay inside LR Ride so seats, payments, and refunds remain traceable.

  • ✓Create one active garage ride at a time.
  • ✓Wait for QR payment confirmation before departure.
  • ✓Mark departed only when boarding is closed.
Privacy

Protect rider data

Rider phone, route, profile, and payment details are for trip handling only.

  • ✓Never share screenshots or trip details outside support.
  • ✓Keep your driver account locked with PIN or biometrics.
  • ✓Report suspicious payment requests immediately.

Emergency protocol

Act fast

Stop in a safe visible place, contact campus security or emergency services, then notify LR Ride support from the app. Preserve trip details and avoid arguments at the scene.

For threats, injury, harassment, vehicle breakdown, or payment fraud, end the ride safely and report with clear notes.

Zero-tolerance actions

  • !Driving under alcohol, drugs, or unsafe fatigue.
  • !Taking riders offline for cash or private payment.
  • !Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or unsafe driving.
  • !Using a different vehicle or account than the approved profile.
