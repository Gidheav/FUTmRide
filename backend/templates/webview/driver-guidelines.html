🛡️ FUTMRIDE Safety & Guidelines

Your complete resource for safe, professional driving, passenger management, vehicle standards, and emergency protocols. All drivers must review these guidelines to maintain platform standing.

🚗

Vehicle Standards

Maintain professional appearance and reliability.

  • ✓ Clean interior & exterior daily
  • ✓ Valid registration & insurance
  • ✓ Regular inspections on schedule
  • ✓ No mechanical warning lights
  • ✓ Functioning AC, heating, lights
😊

Passenger Conduct

Create a respectful, safe environment.

  • ✓ Greet with a smile; confirm destination
  • ✓ Maintain professional demeanor
  • ✓ Safe routes; no aggressive driving
  • ✓ Report inappropriate behavior immediately
  • ✓ Respect personal boundaries
🆘

Emergency Protocols

Know how to respond when incidents occur.

  • ✓ SOS button activates 24/7 support
  • ✓ Campus security number: ext 5555
  • ✓ Medical: Call 911; notify FUTMRIDE
  • ✓ Stay calm; do not engage aggression
  • ✓ Document incidents with photos/notes
📋

Documentation & Verification

Keep records current and accessible.

  • ✓ Driver's license: Valid & current
  • ✓ Vehicle registration: Posted visibly
  • ✓ Insurance: Active coverage on file
  • ✓ Background check: Annual renewal
  • ✓ Photos: Vehicle & you on profile

📍 Route & Navigation Best Practices

\n

Always use FUTMRIDE's in-app routing for current traffic conditions and rider preferences. Avoid shortcuts through residential areas or private property unless specifically directed. Notify riders of delays proactively via in-app chat. Plan for parking at pickup/dropoff locations—don't double-park or block entrances.

\n

Accessibility First: Help elderly, disabled, or mobility-challenged passengers. Wait patiently for pickup of large items or packages. If a rider needs assistance, ask before offering help.

\n
\n

✓ Tip: High ratings come from reliability

\n

Arrive on time, take the fastest safe route, and communicate. Passengers remember drivers who care about their time.

\n\n

🔐 Privacy & Data Security

\n

Protect passenger information. Never share rider details with others or use personal data outside the app. Do not take photos of passengers without explicit written consent. Keep app login credentials private; never share your account.

\n

Payment Security: All payments process through FUTMRIDE. Never ask for cash, digital payment, or personal information directly from riders. Report fraudulent requests immediately to support@futmride.com.

\n
\n

ℹ️ Encryption & Safety

\n

Your location, chats, and transactions are encrypted end-to-end. Protect your phone with a strong PIN or biometric lock.
