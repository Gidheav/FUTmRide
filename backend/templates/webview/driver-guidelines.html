Guidelines & Safety

Best practices for a secure and pleasant ride experience.

Vehicle Maintenance

Ensure your vehicle is clean, well-maintained, and regularly inspected. A safe vehicle is a priority for all CampusDrive trips.

Passenger Conduct

If you encounter inappropriate behavior, please report the incident via the support channel immediately. Your safety is our utmost priority.

Emergency Protocols

In case of an emergency, use the SOS button in the driver app or contact campus security directly. Stay calm and wait for assistance.
