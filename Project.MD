LR Ride – Campus Ride-Hailing System
Project Overview

LR Ride is a dedicated, campus-focused ride-hailing platform designed to provide safe, reliable, and affordable transportation for students within the university environment. The system will connect student passengers with verified drivers operating within the campus axis, ensuring fast pickups, transparent pricing, and secure transactions.

Unlike general ride-hailing platforms, LR Ride is optimized for short-distance travel, high peak periods (e.g., class hours), and a closed-user ecosystem (students and authorized drivers only).

1. Objectives of the System
Provide quick and reliable transportation within campus
Ensure student safety through verified drivers and tracking
Enable cashless and transparent payments
Reduce waiting time and transportation stress
Create a scalable system that can grow across multiple campuses
